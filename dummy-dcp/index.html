Each hospital, on its own patients

After training, each hospital adapts the shared model to its own patients, on site, and keeps whichever version scores best in cross-validation on its own data. The personalized model never leaves the hospital; only these scores come back. Log loss measures how trustworthy the predicted risk percentages are: lower is better.
